Whole-game Teaching & assessment

Safe practice for an unpredictable world combines clear goals with uncertain simulated outcomes. Disruption → Decision → Consequence → Reflection → Articulation is a design process, not an extra assessed student list. Experience creates a reason to learn; explanation, modelling, guided practice and consolidation supply the structure when useful. EDI has a significant role. Productive failure is optional and includes explicit consolidation.
