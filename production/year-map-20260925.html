Planning conclusion

A coherent 30-week allocation can be sketched on paper, using existing experience designs, focused teaching and retained practice. Sufficient teaching time and curriculum mastery are not yet demonstrated. Unit 3 is the tighter half: orientation, sources, applications, megatrends, finance, initiative, diversity and an EST window compete for space. The proposal protects three flexible weeks in Unit 3 and two at year-end, and starts the Unit 4 CPD thread early enough for actual learning and later application. Tania confirms four 50-minute periods weekly; annual EST content and the school assessment outline must be resolved before treating this as a deliverable timetable.

Contact time — confirmed by Tania

Four 50-minute periods per week. Tania asks for a very broad map, allowing for in-class assessments, public holidays/days off and other class requirements, without accounting for every minute. This means 100 nominal hours across 30 full weeks before interruptions, not 100 guaranteed teaching hours. The syllabus gives 110 notional contact hours for the pair (printed p. 2), so this envelope is already 10 hours below that reference before interruptions. This is a feasibility caution, not a new mandated calendar. Keep the map approximately 30 weeks as requested and review real capacity before detailed delivery planning. Protected flexible blocks are part of the allowance; they do not add hours. Their final use remains school-dependent.

Skills taught throughout, not parked in one chapter

The tables locate the principal teaching and evidence homes. Brief explicit skills teaching and supported rehearsal recur inside each unit: research checks when new information is used; communication modelling before responses; task planning and reprioritisation at transitions; independent questioning and solution generation before decisions; teamwork coaching when people collaborate. Unit 3 experience is useful prior knowledge, not a substitute for Unit 4 teaching and evidence.

This recurrence is a new provision proposal, beyond existing one-to-many experience links where necessary. The cross-check labels original mappings versus proposed additions. Software tasks must include documents, presentations and spreadsheets; a game menu alone is not work-related software creation. Communication needs written, verbal, active-listening and observable non-verbal evidence; clicking prepared lines does not suffice.

Timing and assessment constraints

The 2027 syllabus (printed pp. 16–18) specifies at most eight summative tasks, each at least 5%, representative content sampling and each type used. Type totals: investigation 45%; job application/interview 20%; response 20%; EST 15%. Employability skills are integral to tasks. Non-controlled work needs authentication. The EST is written, 50 minutes and invigilated. These are source requirements, not invented school conditions. SCSA syllabus

SCSA's September 2026 circular confirms the 2027 EST administration window: Term 2 Weeks 3–5 and announces selected content from 25 September 2026. The course page retrieved during this task exposed 2026 content only; no 2027 selected-content file was verified. The 2027 handbook is expected in early 2027. This is an access/verification limit, not a claim that the content has not been released. The school's exact sitting, annual content and teaching-week conversion remain open.

Proposed assessment architecture for discussion: adapt rather than add to school tasks. Up to three investigations could draw on W2–8 (research/adaptation/finance), W19–26 (workplace conditions/concerns) and W18–28 (CPD); application and interview evidence could use two linked tasks at W4–5 and W16–18; up to two responses could sample Unit 3 and Unit 4; retain the EST. That is a ceiling of eight, not approval for eight. The school can combine/reduce tasks while preserving required types and weights. No new individual task weights, dates, marking rubrics, recording mandate or duration is set here. As? labels in multiple rows may feed the same task.

Recheck timetable pressure before fixing submissions: investigate first, teach required content, allow feedback, then sample independent performance. If the annual EST selection includes content scheduled late in Unit 3, teach it earlier; do not rely on an opening market encounter or an uncompleted later chapter. Keep the content outside the selection in the full course.

Too many graded artifactsWeek rows must not each become assignments. Select evidence already produced, combine compatible tasks and sample representative learning. Individual understanding still needs evidence when a group produces one shared artifact. No automatic grade from badges, money, votes, support use or number of attempts.
Weak transferRevised responses to the same prompt can reflect memorisation. Deterministic story and valuesA common sequence of learning does not require one winning career, forced unemployment or distress. Characters may make different supported choices; examples of structural barriers should not imply hardship is personal failure. Flourishing, aspiration, enjoyment and assessed knowledge remain distinct.
Theory treated as proofUse the external-perspectives review as design rationale and critique, not endorsement. At each pilot name intended benefit, mechanism, evidence, counter-evidence and retain/revise/remove decision. A simpler non-3D activity may be preferable for some learning.
