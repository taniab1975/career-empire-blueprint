Rewards, accounts and the shared world

Rewards already exist. The Market practice pays $20 gross − $2 community tax = $18 net, a crew pass, Nora’s $5 research fee and an optional $8 Market Rush bonus, plus local saving/spending/donation choices and practice badges. Reflection choices do not change the agreed wage. Preserve these existing distinctions for review; do not add wages for correct answers or approve chance rules here.

The older Career Empire account, economy and teacher-dashboard systems are still in the same game repository. They hold student profiles, salary/net-worth/savings/tax fields, purchases, module progress, evidence reviews and community votes. However, the opening wallet and My Life economy remain separate browser-local practices. Market notes can transfer to local My work with a receipt; that does not make them account-owned assessment evidence. Teacher and community fund views also use differing tax calculations/scopes. These seams must be resolved for the trial.

Review mode: full flourishing world, all teaching/experiences available, synthetic identity and temporary rewards/progress; no writes to real student accounts. Some current local stores already do this, but complete route isolation is unverified. Student trial mode: trusted individual login, owned character/checkpoint, once-only agreed reward/tax updates, return on another/shared device, and authorised teacher visibility. Login and dashboard linkage are trial requirements. Opening everything for review must not open student records or bypass access controls. No new account service, ledger schema or world-development rule is approved by this proposal.
