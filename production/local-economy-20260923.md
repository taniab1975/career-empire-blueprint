## A game about adapting, not accumulating one score

Use parallel outcomes, with no hidden conversion of money into learning achievement:

- **Learning and capability:** evidence, feedback, understanding, strategies attempted and transfer to a new situation. An attempt is not assessed mastery.
- **Completion:** which required experiences/checkpoints have actually been completed; distinct from quality and wealth.
- **Financial position:** available money, savings, earnings, spending, tax and owned assets. Annual salary potential is a rate, not cash in hand.
- **Wellbeing and relationships:** scenario-level energy, time, connection, support and recovery. These are fictional game conditions, not a diagnosis of the student.
- **Contribution and agency:** community decisions, tax, voluntary gifts, volunteering and effects on access or surroundings.

No single combined “good student” score. An affluent character need not be highly skilled, happy or generous. A low-cash character must still be able to learn, seek help, make choices and contribute. Donations must not buy employability marks. Rest must not be penalised as failure. Wellbeing must not become a shopping meter: a purchased green corner creates an affordance, not an automatic happiness award.

Reward can mean a useful clue, an NPC relationship, a new strategy, an enjoyable possession, a repaired place, autonomy, a chance to try again, or pride in a collective outcome. It need not mean money, curriculum progress or higher mastery.

## Productive failure as a playable loop

**World event → attempt → visible consequence → feedback/support → revised attempt → transfer/reflection.**

Example: heat damages a community planting trial. A player invests $100 in an exposed planter. It fails; the plant visibly wilts, the money remains spent, and no completion or mastery is awarded. Inspecting feedback reveals the missing shade. A supported retry supplies reusable materials for free; the player changes the design and sees a shaded, living planter. In a later independent challenge, the player applies the principle elsewhere before mastery can be established. The initial failure becomes productive through feedback, revision and transfer, not merely because failure occurred.

An alternative choice is an extra work shift: wealth rises, but time for recovery and relationships falls. Taking recovery time improves the scenario's wellbeing without paying money or advancing module completion. A later design should expose a finite time budget and competing commitments before selection; avoid teaching that all work damages wellbeing. Context, autonomy, safety, workload and support determine the effect.

Fairness rules: preview stakes; bound losses; no surprise debt spirals; free help and retry routes; accessibility alternatives; no wealth requirement for curriculum; avoid forced public comparisons; no reward farming by repeating the same failure or checkpoint. Failure itself is not a grade deduction. Teacher pacing determines when an event begins or pauses, not the player's bank balance. Do not introduce real-time decay that punishes absence.

## Local state and accounting contract

Production-facing proposal: an anonymous `localPlayerId` per practice slot, a distinct `avatarProfileId` reference, versioned scenario/ruleset, revision, event IDs, explicit activity attempts/completions, evidence references, inventory/placements, and separate financial and life-state projections. No name, email, student ID or class ID is required. Avatar choice does not prove student identity.

The delivered lab is deliberately one practice slot under `career-empire-economy-lab-v1`, storing a versioned event journal. It does not read legacy session/auth keys or avatar profiles. Its pure reducer validates positive integer cents, item IDs, sufficient funds, one-time purchases and completion checkpoints; repeated transaction IDs are idempotent. Reload replays the journal and derives visible consequences. A damaged or unknown-version save is preserved and blocks transactions until explicit reset. Unavailable storage gives a clearly labelled temporary session. Another-tab changes block further actions until reload; simultaneous cross-tab atomicity is not guaranteed by localStorage. Multi-slot state, IndexedDB transactions and migrations belong in the next slice.

Starting practice cash: **$1,000**. A simulated paid checkpoint: **$500 gross − $50 tax = $450 available**. Tax: **10% fictional flat rate**, not an Australian tax model. Desk **$900**, laptop **$1,600**, wellbeing pack **$500** retain old catalog values for comparison. Civic lamp **$150**, planting **$300**, and global kits **$100** are deliberately small test thresholds. The separate old showroom retains **$100,000**. Starting cash and transaction controls are marked test-only; do not infer final economy balance from this sample.

Cash + savings is liquid wealth. Moving money between them conserves value. Purchase expenditure is not automatically the asset's resale value; a future net-worth display needs an explicit valuation/depreciation policy. Tax and voluntary donations are separate ledger entries; fund allocation and later project spending require a further fund ledger before real shared operation. Reward and progress-save notifications must not both pay for one checkpoint.
