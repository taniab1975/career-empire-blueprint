## Stages

1. Check each footprint, entrance and approach independently.
2. Assemble the building/place using the existing material kit.
3. Add its own complete surroundings and props; do not split these into approval items.
4. Check exported geometry, materials and overview/player-height views.
5. Present all ten together for batch approval, with named exceptions.

These are visual production candidates derived from existing services and campus references. New service envelopes are proposals, not mandatory curriculum buildings, new economy rules or fixed map placements. No live runtime, saved evidence or classroom behavior changes. Avatar Studio follows its selected pavilion reference; the nine other items use the campus family. Blazer remains paused.

All ten visual assemblies are built and exported. Separate layout and assembly checks preceded surroundings. The first Studio render exposed overly angular trees; leaf geometry was refined before the final twenty paired overview/walking-height renders. The full-batch contact comparison also prompted distinct laboratory rooflights, a civic lantern/colonnade, a gabled workshop/service door, and corrected garden planting height. The affected four items were re-exported and re-rendered. No appearance acceptance has been recorded.
