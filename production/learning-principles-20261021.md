## Final design direction

Core teaching follows the syllabus: the concept of initiative, the five prescribed ways of showing it, benefits, recognition, explanation and application in actual workplace situations. The five ways are prescribed content/examples, not an ordered taxonomy or a new sequence to memorise. Existing2027curriculum records and their source references remain unchanged.

The retained experience → need to learn → supported instruction → application → feedback/consequence → recovery/transfer sequence remains contextual, not universal. Access, role, safety and workflow support precede an informed attempt. Where suitable, a bounded low-stakes attempt is followed by reflection, explicit explanation and consolidation, supported practice and transfer. Direct teaching or worked examples first remain appropriate when readiness or difficulty requires them. No forced failure, wrong-button penalty or unsupported struggle.

Retrieval practice and spacing are core durable-learning practices. Chunking, meaningful imagery/stories, memory palace, elaboration/self-explanation, words plus meaningful diagrams and interleaving are purpose-specific options. No fixed learning styles or speculative brain mechanisms. Any future optional memory aid must directly reinforce curriculum wording/meaning and demonstrate usefulness, not become another assessed list.

## Explicit Initiative teaching and reduced clutter

The exact ways are: being proactive; identifying ways to improve current work practices; vocalising your opinions; helping fellow workers; seeking more responsibilities. Teach meaning and benefits alongside them: empowerment/recognition, efficiency and work opportunities. Worked examples and non-examples link concrete actions and outcomes to justified syllabus ways, distinguish following instructions and prompted action, and check explanation/application in another workplace case. A wage, badge, opened card, reproduced label or completed quiz is not mastery.

Proposed readable sequence: clear purpose → informed attempt → explicit debrief/meaning/five ways/benefits → worked workplace example → new application and corrective feedback. One primary action per stage, quiet help, deliberate continuation and no auto-advance. Ordinary contextual questions support thinking; no additional named sequence or draft song is introduced to the core experience.
