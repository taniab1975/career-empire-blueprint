# Shared class world: investments, work and curriculum

Confirmed design direction — 24 September 2026. CE-CHANGE-20260924-82.

## What Tania has clarified

The community is the 3D playable world shared by students in the same class. Class contributions progressively improve that world. Personal money and shop purchases belong to each student's Home Base; the shared treasury and class assets are separate. Home Base is a place to display owned purchases, initially with image cards where a suitable 3D item is unavailable, progressively using placed 3D objects. Do not interpret shop ownership as learning attainment.

Shared investment should change both the world and the opportunities within it. Possible projects include repairing existing spaces towards flourishing, saving for a cinema, or green-energy infrastructure that creates changing work opportunities. These are scenario ideas, not a commissioned building list, settled costs or promised jobs. How investment affects Disrepair / Growth / Flourishing remains a design decision; do not make a single contribution automatically advance the entire world or equate wealth with learning.

Cross-school/global learning is deferred until the relevant Department of Education assessment and approval for that scope. Use the existing supplied safety questionnaire and WA assessment workstream (docs/production/wa-assessment-route.md; data/wa-assessment-readiness.json). A completed spreadsheet or local prototype is not approval. Same-class sharing still requires appropriate student identity, class isolation and deployment safeguards. The current lab only simulates funds and is not multiplayer.

## A possible recurring learning sequence

1. A meaningful change creates a need in the shared world.
2. Students investigate the problem and source-check possible responses.
3. Compare projects, costs, access, jobs, skills, benefits and trade-offs. No universally correct purchase.
4. Propose and agree a class priority; a visible progress display shows the simulated funding goal.
5. The chosen investment changes something visible in the world when its agreed conditions are met.
6. New or changing work prompts further investigation, skills development or an application opportunity.
7. Review outcomes and unexpected consequences; revise the plan with support.

Participation, teaching and essential learning access must remain available to students with less money or missed sessions. Do not turn contribution totals into mastery, a popularity contest or a mandatory donation.

## Scenario ideas to explore, not final decisions

- **Repair and flourishing:** restore a shared space; investigate materials, accessibility, environmental effects and maintenance. Determine later whether this changes one site or a town-stage indicator.
- **Cinema:** compare recreation/access benefits, costs and possible construction, hospitality, projection/technical and ongoing operating roles. A cinema appearing is a possible visible payoff, not a current asset commission.
- **Green energy:** compare a solar installation or other suitable infrastructure. Investigate installation versus continuing maintenance, training/pathways and possible running-cost effects. Job numbers, pay, savings and environmental benefits need scenario-specific evidence; avoid guaranteeing them or making all careers available immediately.
