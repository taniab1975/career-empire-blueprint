StatusMeaning
EvidencedThe narrowly stated fact has direct retained evidence. It does not imply whole-question compliance.
Partially evidencedSome evidence supports the position; scope, implementation or operational proof remains incomplete.
GapRequired answer/evidence is missing, or a dated source finding requires resolution. A source gap is not automatically a live vulnerability.
Needs vendor confirmationSupplier-controlled facts, contracts or assurance need current scoped confirmation.
Needs confirmationOwner, assessor or technical confirmation is needed before a truthful answer can be finalised.
