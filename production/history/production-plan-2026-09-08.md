## Performance acceptance remains open

Measure time to first control, bytes before/after entry, decode stalls, sustained frame times, draw calls, visible triangles and decoded texture memory on named representative school devices. Earlier targets (30 fps, 10 MB initial, 200k visible triangles, 100 calls) are proposals, not established compliance. The current 120k Home Base alone is about 10.2 MB and its reported public sample was 29 fps. Compare quality and performance under matched conditions before deciding LOD and texture budgets. Do not impose blanket 1K maps or drop shader connections to make a number look good.
