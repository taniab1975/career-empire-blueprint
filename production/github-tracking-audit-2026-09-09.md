A Projects board is optional; it should display those same issues rather than become another manually maintained masterplan.

Triage the nine open issues as retain/rewrite, implemented-but-needs-verification, superseded, or still-unapproved proposal. Preserve original wording and rationale in history. Do not close a ticket as completed merely because it is old. Each retained issue should link its Blueprint record ID, owner, dependencies and acceptance checks; the Blueprint should link back to the issue and record the material outcome. Old plans should carry a prominent historical/superseded pointer; AGENTS.md and README.md should direct new sessions to the current Blueprint.

For now, avoid another board and duplicate checklists. No automatic Blueprint-to-Issues synchronisation is configured. GitHub's native Issues/Projects synchronisation does not automatically maintain the Blueprint.
