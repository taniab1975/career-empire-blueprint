Domain rationale and source distinction — 25 September 2026

Tania confirms each domain's design choices must link to the underpinning knowledge, skills and rationale, distinct from the student's sequential experience and required curriculum learning. Within existing records, connect: experience/week and domain -> concrete design choice -> relevant source/principle or requirement -> explanation of its application -> intended learner knowledge/skill where applicable -> evidence/review of whether it works. Distinguish designer knowledge from student learning outcomes. Distinguish mandatory requirements, Department assessment questions/guidance, research findings, theoretical/design frameworks, project preferences and untested assumptions; a citation alone does not validate a feature. Motivation/game design, hero's journey/drama and constructivist/direct-instruction approaches inform choices; curriculum defines required student learning. WA assessment sources inform data/response design and other applicable security/vendor/identity requirements, not just text boxes. Teacher reporting should explain the instructional decision each measure supports; engagement/activity, correctness, answer quality and transfer are separate indicators, not interchangeable proof of mastery. Use shared source references and specific per-choice rationale rather than duplicate theory essays or create another master plan. This records design direction only: no completed source mapping, new research validation, week sequence, software, collection, visual asset or public publication is claimed.

Sequential year journey clarification — 24 September 2026

Tania specifies an approximately 30-teaching-week sequence shaped by the hero's journey, beginning at the start of the avatar's life in Career Empire. The megatrend/Jobs Centre example belongs within that developing journey, not automatically at its beginning. Map each week/chapter across student experience, places/buildings, decisions, consequences/rewards, curriculum and learning evidence, teacher view, and privacy/AI constraints. Pedagogy, engagement, educational psychology, drama/story and curriculum rationale inform these design decisions. Curriculum remains a required design constraint as well as a mapped domain; do not fit it retrospectively to an already fixed story. Exact weeks, chapter boundaries, hero-cycle interpretation and assessment sequence remain unapproved. Proposed layout: year overview, expandable weekly/chapter domain map, linked rationale/evidence. Preserve existing records and approved experience work; no new catalogue, game implementation or public publication by this clarification. Next design step is a broad beginning-to-end arc checked against curriculum timing before detailed week planning. No new visual assets.

Finding

The Blueprint already contains a coherent educational idea: circumstances create a reason to learn, students seek information and teaching, act, receive feedback and adapt. The curriculum design page is part of that foundation. Buildings can provide authentic services within these experiences. The gap is not a lack of ideas; it is that the connections between the experience designs, learning modes, evidence, rewards and implementation are not consistently specified or presented.

The Blueprint is the game/learning-design document now. A teacher resource should later be derived from this design. Curriculum defines required learning; pedagogy, educational psychology and engagement shape how students encounter and develop it. Privacy, authenticity, accessibility and scale constrain and test design decisions. These are related responsibilities, not competing educational purposes.

Unit 3 applications and Unit 4 interviews/lifelong learning should not be collapsed into one early lesson solely to make this story tidy. The journey can recur across the year: an initial encounter, later teaching, then more demanding application.

The same Jobs Centre can support an ambitious student seeking promotion, a returning worker or a student changing priorities. Unemployment need not be the only trigger. This makes the place reusable and keeps learning connected to circumstances.

Other useful connections

Class investment: changed local work → research community needs → compare investment proposals → reasoned class decision → new service or opportunity → new work/teamwork experience → review effects. Retain individual reasons as appropriate; winning a vote is not the assessed outcome.

EST practice: identify a knowledge gap or scheduled revision need → direct practice in an existing activity → specific feedback → retry or teaching → later application in a relevant task. Any game earnings should have a clear, bounded rule and be separate from the learning judgement. No artificial world crisis is needed to justify every revision session.

Learning evidence: chosen action or constructed plan → consequence → targeted explanation/revision → teacher review or independent demonstration → curriculum claim. Decide the minimum necessary retained evidence before building storage. Student performance records can still be personal information even without student email addresses.

Recommended way forward: connect before expanding

Use the existing curriculum/experience records as the design home. Do not add another master plan.

First reconcile the small set of conflicting “current” statements. Then trace one complete journey across the existing records using five questions:

  1. What changed or what does the student want?
  2. What must they understand or be able to do, and what teaching/support helps?
  3. Where do they act, what carries between modes, and what consequence follows?
  4. What would demonstrate learning here, and later in a changed context?
  5. What must be retained, who reviews it, and what is the next step?

For each connection distinguish designed, implemented, verified in use, and learning evidence reviewed. These are separate states, not one overall green light. Privacy readiness also remains separate from educational quality.

A bounded review of the megatrend-to-Jobs-Centre journey would expose the missing joins without committing to a new building, large system or whole-year rewrite. Only after that should it become an implementation plan. This recommendation is not a new production approval.
