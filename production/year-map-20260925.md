## Contact time — confirmed by Tania

## Reading the week tables

Weeks are **ordinal teaching allocations**, not dates or term-week conversions. W1–15 = proposed Semester 1 / Unit 3; W16–30 = proposed Semester 2 / Unit 4. All allocations are proposals. Confirmed external timing is stated separately below.

`I` introduce · `T` explicitly teach/model · `P` practise with feedback · `A` apply · `R` revisit/retrieve · `As?` candidate formal assessment opportunity, subject to the school's approved outline. An `As?` label does not create a new graded task. Short evidence checks are formative unless approved otherwise. Curriculum prefixes are shortened only in these tables: **U3-… = CEM12-U3-…; U4-… = CEM12-U4-…**. Inclusive numbered ranges enumerate exact ledger IDs; the cross-check expands every point and nested bullet. “All U3/U4” in consolidation means diagnose/select relevant previously taught points, not assess every point at once.

Modes: **World** = bounded encounter/choice/consequence; **Teach** = explicit teaching, worked examples and supported practice, inside or outside 3D; **Dedicated** = existing learning/software/EST spaces; **Observed** = teacher-supported individual or small-group performance. Alternative non-3D cases preserve learning and re-entry for absent students.

## Skills taught throughout, not parked in one chapter

The tables locate the principal teaching and evidence homes. Brief explicit skills teaching and supported rehearsal recur inside **each** unit: research checks when new information is used; communication modelling before responses; task planning and reprioritisation at transitions; independent questioning and solution generation before decisions; teamwork coaching when people collaborate. Unit 3 experience is useful prior knowledge, not a substitute for Unit 4 teaching and evidence.

This recurrence is a **new provision proposal**, beyond existing one-to-many experience links where necessary. The cross-check labels original mappings versus proposed additions. Software tasks must include documents, presentations and spreadsheets; a game menu alone is not work-related software creation. Communication needs written, verbal, active-listening and observable non-verbal evidence; clicking prepared lines does not suffice.

## Timing and assessment constraints

The 2027 syllabus (printed pp. 16–18) specifies **at most eight summative tasks**, each at least 5%, representative content sampling and each type used. Type totals: investigation 45%; job application/interview 20%; response 20%; EST 15%. Employability skills are integral to tasks. Non-controlled work needs authentication. The EST is written, 50 minutes and invigilated. These are source requirements, not invented school conditions. [SCSA syllabus](https://senior-secondary.scsa.wa.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/1262159/Careers-and-Employability-General-Year-12-Syllabus-for-teaching-from-2027.PDF)

SCSA's [September 2026 circular](https://www.scsa.wa.edu.au/7to12-circulars/edition-9-september-2026) confirms the **2027 EST administration window: Term 2 Weeks 3–5** and announces selected content from 25 September 2026. The course page retrieved during this task exposed 2026 content only; no 2027 selected-content file was verified. The 2027 handbook is expected in early 2027. This is an access/verification limit, not a claim that the content has not been released. The school's exact sitting, annual content and teaching-week conversion remain open.

**Proposed assessment architecture for discussion:** adapt rather than add to school tasks. Up to three investigations could draw on W2–8 (research/adaptation/finance), W19–26 (workplace conditions/concerns) and W18–28 (CPD); application and interview evidence could use two linked tasks at W4–5 and W16–18; up to two responses could sample Unit 3 and Unit 4; retain the EST. That is a ceiling of eight, not approval for eight. The school can combine/reduce tasks while preserving required types and weights. No new individual task weights, dates, marking rubrics, recording mandate or duration is set here. `As?` labels in multiple rows may feed **the same** task.

Recheck timetable pressure before fixing submissions: investigate first, teach required content, allow feedback, then sample independent performance. If the annual EST selection includes content scheduled late in Unit 3, teach it earlier; do not rely on an opening market encounter or an uncompleted later chapter. Keep the content outside the selection in the full course.
