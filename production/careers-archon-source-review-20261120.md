# Careers Archon

Careers Archon brings Initiative learning into a Perth-inspired 3D strategy game. Two students lead rival restaurant teams; a third student adjudicates. Players answer the same workplace question privately, discuss both responses using the Initiative guide, then apply the verdict and choose a lasting territory rule. Team-coloured staff pieces, restaurant upgrades and a clear match result make progress visible.

Verified checks:438 unit tests,19 targeted game checks, and desktop/phone checks for opening Archon from the Market and returning with learning state, focus and movement preserved. These checks do not establish performance or classroom-device readiness.

Release inventory: 442 asset hashes and 111 local modules checked. Supporting Blueprint review only; game publication is a separate guarded step.
