## Gameplay, economy, progression and class experience

The browser prototype provides walking, camera views, Studio/profile controls, destinations and manual phase preview. It does not establish a complete earned life-development loop. Disrepair → Growth → Flourishing expresses unrealised potential and communal growth; the manual selector is not earned class progress, and disrepair must not communicate personal deficiency or deliberately poor visual quality.

Tax from simulated earnings funding class improvements is approved. The older 10% setting is implementation history, not a final rate. Earning sources, costs, treasury spending authority, stage transitions, carry-over, uneven progress and absence remain unresolved. Existing shared economy events are useful reuse candidates; awarding a reward and logging its saved state must not duplicate money. Validate event identity/replay behavior rather than deriving totals from unrelated fields.

Individual “I am becoming”, class “We built this” and future global cooperation remain distinct layers. Same-class visibility and earned appearance are a retained future requirement. Synchronous multiplayer is not a dependency for the first build; asynchronous echoes are a possible implementation, not automatic acceptance. Trading, chat, rankings, global participation and competitive modes require separate decisions. Inspiration, contribution and chosen achievement precede wealth comparison. Salary penalties, a composite wellbeing score and fixed relationships/health values are not approved by the newer brief’s list of possible consequences.

Buildings house services and experiences; old names do not mandate construction. The four legacy transformation tracks, companions, entertainment venues and a dedicated lifelong-learning campaign remain labelled candidates/open decisions. Original inside jokes and discovery are retained; the movie anecdote does not authorise franchise reproduction. Required learning may not depend on accidental discovery, cosmetic purchase or irreversible failure.

Teacher orchestration must support mixed progress, interruption, late arrivals, absence, pause/resume and alternatives to personal disclosure or paid-work experience. Active time is captured activity time, not attendance or learning quality. Separate objective correctness, written-answer review, evidence ownership and summative assessment. These requirements exist; a complete classroom workflow has not been accepted.
