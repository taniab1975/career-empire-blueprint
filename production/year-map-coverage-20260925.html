Content authority and freedom to improve the teaching — Tania confirmed 27 September 2026

The required content is non-negotiable; the textbook activities are optional. SCSA specifies what must be covered. Carolan is a coherent reference for the depth, breadth, concepts and terminology that students need to learn, not a prescribed sequence, script or activity bank. There is no requirement to use the textbook or reproduce its activities. A chapter/page link in this plan records the content/depth cross-check; it does not assign that worksheet to students or require every textbook extension.

Career Empire is intended to improve the learning resource. Teach the required content in original, clearer ways through modelling, purposeful game decisions, feedback and repeated application. Replace weak or unsuitable textbook activities freely while preserving the intended learning and checking it at the required depth. Do not merely decorate a worksheet with a game reward, and do not treat entertaining exposure as a substitute for teaching.

Do not improvise coverage from arbitrary resources. Start each design with the exact SCSA point and named content students must learn; cross-check Carolan's treatment and then justify any supplementation or replacement with relevant, credible, dated sources. Use current primary/official sources for changing facts, legislation and labour-market data. Record the source, supported claim, missing depth or disagreement, and how the game teaches and checks that content. Source choice and activity quality are design judgements; an unresolved curriculum/content conflict must stay visible rather than being guessed away. No approval is required merely to discard a poor textbook activity within the authorised design scope.
