Approve the whole building or scene — not every wall or flower.

For example: a building already assembled with its component parts. Your overall approval includes those parts unless you flag an exception. A batch can contain up to ten whole items.

How to give feedback

For example: “Approve the Avatar Studio, except change the entrance sign.” You do not need to approve the remaining parts individually.

We only return something for appearance review if you request changes or a later change materially alters the approved look. The complete assembly must exist before whole-item approval. Approval does not automatically start the next production stage.

Approvals are recorded in the canonical Blueprint after your explicit review. Component details are supporting information; there is no separate approval task for every part. This page does not save approvals in your browser.
